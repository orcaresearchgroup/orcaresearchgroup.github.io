About Me

I am a postdoctoral scholar at the Center for Ocean-Atmospheric Prediction Studies (COAPS) at Florida State University. I completed my PhD at the Indian Institute of Technology Bhubaneswar.

COAPS profile

Research Interests

I study how winds affect coastal ocean circulation and upwelling, the process that brings deeper water toward the surface. My work in the western Bay of Bengal examines how winds along the coast and winds farther away influence upwelling.

Selected Publications

  • A systematic method of estimation of alongshore windstress and Ekman transport associated with coastal upwelling · MethodsX, 2023
  • Coastal Upwelling in the Western Bay of Bengal: Role of Local and Remote Windstress · Remote Sensing, 2022
