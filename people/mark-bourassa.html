About Me

I am a professor of meteorology in Florida State University’s Department of Earth, Ocean and Atmospheric Science and associate director of the Center for Ocean-Atmospheric Prediction Studies (COAPS).

I lead the NASA Ocean Vector Wind Science Team and contribute to the Shipboard Automated Meteorological and Oceanographic Systems (SAMOS) working group.

COAPS faculty profile

Research Interests

I study how the ocean, atmosphere, and surface waves influence one another, including how energy and momentum cross the air–sea interface. My work also covers remote sensing of surface winds, fluxes, and precipitation, and how to combine observations from different sources.

I pair satellite and in situ observations with models of the oceanic and atmospheric boundary layers to study tropical disturbances, surface wind and flux variability, and how ocean conditions affect the atmosphere.
