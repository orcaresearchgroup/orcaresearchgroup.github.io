Ilkyeong Ma

Ilkyeong Ma is a postdoctoral research associate at the Center for Ocean-Atmospheric Prediction Studies (COAPS) at Florida State University. He uses mesoscale ocean simulations to study how energy sinks such as bottom drag and ocean current feedback affect ocean dynamics and the eddy kinetic energy budget.
