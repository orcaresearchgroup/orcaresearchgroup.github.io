About Me

I am a physical oceanographer from Spain. I earned a master’s degree in integrated coastal zone management at the University of Cantabria and a PhD in physical oceanography at CICESE in Mexico. I went on to study coastal hydrodynamics at ENS Paris-Saclay and joined France Energies Marines in 2025.

France Energies Marines team directory

Research Interests

I study how changing winds generate surface waves and currents and how momentum and energy move across the air–sea interface. My work has included wind–wave experiments, numerical modeling of waves under hurricane winds, and how breaking waves contribute to coastal erosion. In France, I also work on modeling the wakes of offshore wind farms.

Selected Research

  • Surface drift currents and coupling at the ocean–atmosphere interface · PhD thesis, CICESE, 2019
  • Wave spectra and turbulent energy dissipation under accelerating winds · EGU General Assembly, 2020
