Olmo Zavala

Olmo Zavala Romero is an assistant professor in the Department of Scientific Computing at Florida State University, where he leads the PULSE Lab. He previously worked as a research scientist at FSU’s Center for Ocean-Atmospheric Prediction Studies (COAPS) and earned his PhD in computational science at FSU.

Personal website and CV

Research Interests

Olmo develops machine learning and high-performance computing methods for earth science and medicine. His ocean research uses neural networks to incorporate observations into circulation models, estimate subsurface temperature and salinity from satellite data, and predict Loop Current behavior and eddies in the Gulf of Mexico. He also uses particle-tracking models to study marine litter transport and forecast oil spill trajectories.
