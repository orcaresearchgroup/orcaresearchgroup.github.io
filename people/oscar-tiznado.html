About Me

I am an undergraduate student at the Faculty of Marine Sciences at Universidad Autónoma de Baja California (UABC) in Mexico.

Research Interests

I use coupled numerical simulations to study how feedback from ocean currents to the atmosphere changes the properties and spatial distribution of eddies in the Caribbean Sea.
