About Me

I am a professor in the Faculty of Marine Sciences (Facultad de Ciencias Marinas, FCM) at Universidad Autónoma de Baja California (UABC) in Ensenada, Mexico. I advise oceanology students on research in coastal and regional oceanography.

UABC Faculty of Marine Sciences directory

Research Interests

I study coastal circulation and how oceanographic conditions vary across locations and seasons. My work includes coastal water quality in Todos Santos Bay and how the interaction between eddies and the larger-scale flow in the Caribbean Sea is influenced by feedback from ocean currents to the atmosphere.

Selected Research

  • Spatial and temporal variations in water quality of Todos Santos Bay, northwestern Baja California, Mexico · Marine Pollution Bulletin, 2021
  • Eddy-mean flow interactions in the Caribbean Sea and their modulation by current feedback to the atmosphere · Preprint, 2026
